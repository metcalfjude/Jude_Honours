\chapter{Functional Advantages of Visual Search Dynamics}\label{chap:functional_advantages}
Now that we have developed and validated a rigorous model of the dynamics of gaze across all spatiotemporal scales, we investigate what we can learn from the efficiency of the model to an informative search task. The first section of this chapter (\cref{sec:functional_roles_of_dynamical_patterns}) presents informational-theoretic methods which have been constructed to analyse efficiency during abstract search tasks. This method takes the framework introduced in \cref{chap:a_unifying_model}, and using the information encoded in kernel dynamics, quantifies the information of a belief distribution as the distribution entropy and tracks its development through a search task. We take these metrics and compare the informational advantages of each dynamical pattern by comparing an abstract sampler operating in different points in parameter space $\alpha/\beta/\gamma/\tau$. The final section of the main body of this thesis (\cref{sec:further_extensions_of_studied_dynamic}) examines further extensions of these dynamics beyond the case of a 2-dimensional visual search to high-dimensional or dynamical target landscapes, where control and exploration are hypothesised be more responsive and robust to shifts in a loss function.
